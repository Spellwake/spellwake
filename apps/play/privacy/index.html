Privacy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy covers Spellwake’s 0.1.0 tabletop preview, published by Aaron Cassar under the Spellwake name, and the preview website at app.spellwake.com.

The tabletop preview

The preview displays bundled sample cards and a six-player table. It has no account sign-in, live multiplayer, purchases, advertising or analytics SDKs. Card artwork and fonts are bundled in the native app. Your card selections, hand browsing and table interactions are not sent to a Spellwake gameplay server.

The preview does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Testing and diagnostics

Apple, Google and your operating system process installation and diagnostic information under their own policies. Beta testing can share information with us so we can investigate problems and improve reliability.

TestFlight shares tester information, including your name and email when you join by email invitation, app usage, crash information and feedback you choose to send. Google Play provides testing, app-quality and crash reports. Reports can contain device and operating-system information. These platform services are separate from the preview’s bundled tabletop interactions.

See TestFlight & Privacy, Google’s Privacy Policy and your device’s diagnostic settings for their practices and available controls.

Website hosting

When you visit app.spellwake.com, our hosting and delivery providers, Render and Cloudflare, process network information such as your IP address and requested resources to deliver and protect the website. We do not add advertising trackers or an analytics SDK to the preview website.

Provider processing is described in Render’s Privacy Policy and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

Contact aaronncassar@gmail.com for support or privacy questions, including requests to access, correct or delete information you have sent us. We receive your email address and whatever information you include, and use it to handle your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or identity documents.

GitHub Issues are public. If you choose to report a bug there, your submission is processed by GitHub and is visible to others. Use email for personal or sensitive requests. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Use, sharing and retention

We use support correspondence and available testing diagnostics to respond to requests, investigate faults and maintain the preview. We do not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising. Hosting, email and testing providers process information as part of their services; information may also be disclosed where required by law.

We keep support correspondence and any diagnostic copies we retain only as needed to address requests, maintain reliability or meet applicable obligations. Provider-held information follows the relevant provider’s retention rules. You can email us to request deletion of information we hold; we may need enough information to verify and fulfill the request.

Changes

This policy describes the current preview. We will update it when our practices change, including before introducing connected accounts, live gameplay or purchases. The effective date above identifies the current version.
