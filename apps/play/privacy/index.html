Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Aaron Cassar operates Spellwake. This policy covers the connected tabletop at app.spellwake.com, the Spellwake apps, and the support we provide for them. Some features are available only in certain builds or to invited testers.

Accounts and sign-in

We process your email address or an identifier from your chosen sign-in provider, your username, and information about your account and sessions. Spellwake offers one-time email codes and sign-in through Google, Apple, or Discord. Supabase provides authentication. We use these details to sign you in, maintain your account, and investigate unauthorized access. Your chosen provider handles its own sign-in under its privacy policy.

Play, profiles, and communication

When you use connected features, we store the decks, collections, settings, match actions, results, and replay data needed to run them. We also process chat, messages, friend and block lists, and reports when you use those features. Other players can see your public profile, messages you send them, and match information that the game makes available to them based on their role in the match.

If you play against a hosted AI opponent, Spellwake may send gameplay context and instructions to OpenAI to choose the opponent's actions. We also track hosted AI usage to apply daily match allowances.

Purchases and supporter memberships

Where cosmetic checkout is available, Stripe handles payment details. Spellwake receives the identifiers, amounts, and statuses needed to track purchases, grant cosmetics, and handle refunds. Enter card details only in Stripe checkout.

Patreon handles supporter memberships and membership payments. If you link Patreon to Spellwake, we process membership identifiers, tier and status, and records needed to reconcile supporter benefits. If you separately connect Discord through Patreon, Patreon and Discord use that connection to manage supporter roles and channel access.

Hosting, security, and diagnostics

Render, Cloudflare, and Fly.io help deliver and protect the service. Supabase provides the database and storage. These services process information needed to operate Spellwake, including network and request information. We use session, security, and error records to maintain the service, recover matches, and investigate bugs or abuse. Spellwake does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

If you install or test a native app, Apple, Google, or your operating system may provide installation information, crash reports, device details, and feedback you choose to send. See TestFlight & Privacy and Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account and gameplay information as needed to provide the service. We retain security, payment, match, and support records as needed to resolve disputes, preserve shared match history, reconcile payments, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Providers also retain records under their own policies.

You can request deletion in Account settings or by email. We may need to verify the request and settle outstanding activity before completing it. Some shared match or financial records may be kept for the reasons above. You can also contact us to request access to or correction of information we hold about you.

Contact

Email aaronncassar@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. We use the information you send to respond. GitHub Issues are public. Use email for private information and keep sign-in codes and financial details out of support requests.

Changes

The effective date above identifies this version. We update this page when the way Spellwake handles information changes.
